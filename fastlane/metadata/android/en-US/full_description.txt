Photos and screenshots say more than you think: where you were, what phone
you own, sometimes a hidden copy of the uncropped shot or a video clip
appended by motion-photo mode.

Sepia is the step before you hit send:

* X-ray: see everything the file says about you, ranked by severity. GPS in
  plain words, the hidden thumbnail shown, trailing data called out.
* Redact: ink and pixelation burned into the pixels, plus crop. Where the device supports code
  detection, QR codes are found and offered for covering.
* Prove it: the export is re-encoded from scratch, then Sepia re-opens its
  own output and shows you what is (and is not) still inside.

Everything happens on your device. This app has no INTERNET permission: the
operating system will not let it open a connection, and you can check the
manifest instead of trusting a promise.

No accounts, no analytics, no dependencies. MIT licensed.
