Privacy

Sepia has no server side. There is nothing to write a long policy about, so here is the whole thing:

  • Your images are processed on your device and never uploaded. The page opens no connections except to load its own files from this site. The Android app ships without the internet permission, so it cannot open connections at all.
  • Sepia keeps no accounts, sets no analytics, uses no cookies, and embeds nothing from anyone else.
  • The web app stores one thing in your browser: your language choice. The Android app keeps your most recent shared export in its private cache so the receiving app can read it; that file is cleared on the next share and on every app start.
  • When you share an image into the installed web app, your browser briefly parks the file in local storage to hand it to the page, which deletes it on pickup. The Android app hands bytes over in memory instead.
